## 📌 Project Overview

**DRISHTI** is an intelligent real-time monitoring and inspection platform that leverages Artificial Intelligence to enable transparent inspections, verified evidence collection, anomaly detection, and end-to-end institutional accountability.

The platform digitizes the inspection process through a mobile application that enables inspectors to conduct inspections, capture GPS-based and photographic evidence, submit reports, and monitor inspection activities in real time.

DRISHTI combines mobile-based inspection, AI-powered analysis, real-time monitoring, alerts, reporting, and compliance auditing into a unified platform.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Enable **real-time monitoring and smart inspections**.
- Reduce fake or unverifiable inspection reporting.
- Collect **verified and time-stamped evidence**.
- Use AI to identify anomalies and potential risks.
- Improve transparency and accountability.
- Digitize inspection reports and records.
- Enable faster identification and tracking of irregularities.
- Support institutional compliance and auditing.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Traditional inspection and monitoring processes often rely on manual reporting, physical documentation, photographs, and delayed communication.

This can result in:

- Fake or unverifiable reporting
- Delayed inspection updates
- Lack of reliable evidence
- Difficulty detecting irregularities
- Limited real-time visibility
- Difficulty tracking inspection activities
- Lack of end-to-end accountability

DRISHTI addresses these challenges through a digital, AI-enabled, real-time inspection and monitoring platform.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

DRISHTI provides a centralized platform for conducting, verifying, monitoring, and analyzing inspections.

The solution uses:

- 📍 GPS-based location verification
- 📸 Photographic evidence collection
- ⏱️ Time-stamped inspection records
- 🤖 AI-powered anomaly detection
- 🚨 Risk and anomaly alerts
- 📊 Real-time monitoring
- 📝 Digital inspection reports
- 📋 Compliance auditing
- 📈 Analytics and dashboards

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Real-Time Monitoring & Smart Inspections

Inspectors can conduct field inspections through the mobile application and submit inspection information digitally.

### 2. AI-Powered Anomaly Detection

AI-based processing helps identify irregularities and potential risks from inspection data and visual evidence.

### 3. Verified Evidence Collection

The application captures supporting evidence such as:

- GPS coordinates
- Photographs
- Inspection details
- Time-stamped records

This improves the reliability and traceability of inspection reports.

### 4. Risk & Anomaly Alerts

Potential irregularities detected during monitoring can generate alerts for further investigation.

### 5. Digital Reporting

Inspection information is stored digitally and can be used for generating reports and analytics.

### 6. Compliance Audit

Inspection records can be used to monitor compliance and support auditing.

### 7. End-to-End Accountability

The inspection workflow can be tracked from assignment and execution to submission, analysis, reporting, and follow-up.
